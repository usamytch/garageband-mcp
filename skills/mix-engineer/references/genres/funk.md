# Funk / disco-funk

**Aim:** a clear rhythmic conversation between drums, bass and short guitar/keyboard parts. Preserve the pocket and intentional dynamics.

**Working hypotheses:** establish bass-note duration and kick relationship before reaching for heavy compression. Listen to ghost notes, muted guitar attacks and offbeat accents in context. Try restrained dynamics control only when it improves consistency without flattening the groove. Check that ambience leaves the spaces between notes audible.

**Compare:** whether the bass remains melodic, the backbeat moves naturally and the arrangement still breathes at low volume. Check mono for cancellations between rhythmic layers.

**Avoid:** quantizing a human pocket by default, making every ghost note prominent, or treating all funk as an imitation of one production era.

**Source observation:** the Mick Guzauski/Peter Franco interview about Daft Punk's *Random Access Memories* describes source tone, groove and restrained processing in that particular production. [Sound On Sound, 2013](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/recording-random-access-memories-daft-punk).
