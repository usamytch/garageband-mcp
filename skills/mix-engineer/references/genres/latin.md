# Latin: choose the tradition first

**Initial coverage:** Latin pop/reggaeton. “Latin” is a family, not one drum pattern or mix chain. For salsa, bachata, samba and other traditions, establish appropriate references before applying this profile's urban-pop assumptions.

**Working hypotheses:** identify the rhythmic anchor and each percussion role. Keep kick/bass weight from swallowing syncopated details. Fit vocal phrasing around the groove; check that rides preserve articulation and musical accents. Use width and ambience only when the percussion's timing and mono clarity survive.

**Compare:** groove identity, percussion separation, lead presence and low-end translation. A technically cleaner balance that weakens the style's character is not an automatic improvement.

**Avoid:** imposing a reggaeton low-end treatment on acoustic Latin ensembles or claiming one famous engineer's method covers all regional traditions.

**Source scope:** the public overview of Jaycen Joshua's [Rosalía — Yo x Ti](https://mixwiththemasters.com/videos/jaycen-joshua-rosalia-yo-x-ti/part/0) course lists percussion, low-end and vocal-processing topics. The paid lesson was not accessed; no detailed technique or parameter claims are derived from it. Provenance: `joshua-rosalia` in [sources.json](../sources.json).
