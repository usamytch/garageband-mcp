# Pop

**Aim:** an unmistakable lead and hook, coherent low end and deliberate contrast between sections. Establish the production's identity before adding polish.

**Working hypotheses:** balance the lead against the busiest hook section, then use modest phrase rides to retain words and intentional dynamic shape. Distinguish a lead double, harmony and texture before changing levels. Check whether bass movement, pumping or distortion is already printed into a source; avoid processing away the intended pattern.

**Compare:** hook recognition, intelligibility at quiet volume, breath/consonant naturalness and the transition into the chorus. Match perceived loudness when deciding whether additional compression helps.

**Avoid:** a compulsory streaming loudness target, indiscriminate de-essing, or stripping characteristic production effects merely because a solo sounds unusual.

**Source observation:** Ian Kirkpatrick's account of producing Dua Lipa's [Don't Start Now](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-dua-lipa-dont-start-now) (Sound On Sound, 2020) gives a song-specific production context. It is not a generic mixing recipe or a Josh Gudwin interview. Indexed excerpts were accessible. Provenance: `kirkpatrick-2020` in [sources.json](../sources.json).
