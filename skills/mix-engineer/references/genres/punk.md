# Punk / pop-punk

**Aim:** direct impact, readable vocal, purposeful guitar density and a rhythm section that keeps its attack. Distinguish raw punk from polished pop-punk in the brief.

**Working hypotheses:** balance kick, snare and bass against the guitars before brightening everything. Give competing guitars distinct roles through performance, level or pan; do not manufacture width without checking mono. Try small vocal rides where dense refrains hide syllables. Preserve aggression without making every hit equally loud.

**Compare:** chorus lift, snare attack at quiet playback, vocal intelligibility and upper-mid fatigue at matched loudness. If a polished treatment removes the intended roughness, reject it.

**Avoid:** automatic drum replacement, maximum compression on every bus, or copying an engineer's plugin list without the corresponding sources and goal.

**Source observation:** Tom Lord-Alge discusses upfront drums and his work with Sum 41, source-dependent processing and respecting the band's intent in [Inside the Mix](https://www.waves.com/inside-the-mix-tom-lord-alge) (2017). This profile's experiments are our synthesis, not his preset. Provenance: `tla-2017` in [sources.json](../sources.json).
