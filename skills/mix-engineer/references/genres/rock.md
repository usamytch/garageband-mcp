# Rock

**Aim:** a convincing band balance with deliberate energy and a lead that remains connected to the performance. Clarify whether the desired result is roomy, intimate, raw or tightly produced.

**Working hypotheses:** establish a rough full-band balance quickly, then make changes in context. Use guitar level/pan and vocal rides to expose the song before adding more processing. Revisit drum attack and sustain at contrasting playback levels. Preserve the difference between verses, choruses and climaxes.

**Compare:** emotional impact, vocal continuity, guitar/drum competition, mono coherence and whether a quieter section still feels intentionally quieter.

**Avoid:** copying an entire processing chain before hearing the source or treating the loudest candidate as the winner.

**Source observation:** Andy Wallace describes contextual balancing, fader rides and listening decisions in the interview about Linkin Park's [The Hunting Party](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/inside-track-linkin-parks-hunting-party) (Sound On Sound, 2014). Accessible indexed interview passages support those principles; no exact settings are prescribed here. Provenance: `wallace-2014` in [sources.json](../sources.json).
